Pocket Collider

Privacy policy

ChilliCactus Games

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. ABOUT THIS POLICY

Pocket Collider is a particle-collider idle game published by ChilliCactus Games ("we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled in the Android version of the game.

You do not need an account to play, and the game does not send your gameplay records to us. The game shows advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob, and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. It contains no analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game saves information locally so that you can continue playing and keep your preferences. This includes:

  • Collected resources, upgrade levels and your collider's energy.
  • Discovered particles, when they were found and which particle entries you have viewed.
  • Collider charge, cooldown and the time of the next automatic experiment.
  • The scientists in your lab, collision counts and other progress totals.
  • The last time the game was open and the latest time it has recorded, used to run automatic experiments while you are away.
  • A random-number value that decides collision results, so that reopening the game does not change them.
  • A count of collisions since the last full-screen advert, which of our own games is promoted next, and how many reward videos you have watched today.
  • Whether you have made a purchase that removes adverts, and a record of the purchases already delivered, so that none is delivered twice.
  • Your reduced-motion, sound and music preferences.

This information is stored locally within the Android app. It is used to run the game, restore progress, calculate automatic experiments, pace adverts and remember settings. It is not sent to a ChilliCactus Games server.

Automatic experiments are calculated on your device from its clock, for up to 48 hours while the game is closed. The game does not contact a server to run experiments or record results. There are no online accounts, public leaderboards or developer-operated cloud saves.

3. DEVICE ACCESS AND CONNECTIONS

The Android app uses an internet connection to request and show adverts and to handle purchases through Google Play. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or messages. Game files and artwork are loaded locally, and the game itself can be played without a connection.

4. ADVERTISING

The game shows a banner advert on the main screen, a full-screen advert when the game starts and, from time to time, a full-screen advert after a collision. You can also choose to watch a reward video in exchange for in-game resources. Reward videos are optional and are not required to play.

Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver adverts, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with adverts. Google's use of this information is described at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it, including in the UK and the European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, using Google's consent tools. If you do not consent, you may still see adverts, but they are not personalised to you. You can change your choice later from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised adverts, in your Android device's settings.

When an advert cannot be loaded, the game may instead show a promotion for one of our other games. These promotions are built into the app, and no information is sent when they are shown. If you tap one, the game opens that title's Google Play page in the Google Play Store app or, if that is not available, in your web browser. Google's terms and privacy policy apply from that point.

We do not receive your advertising ID or other information that identifies you from adverts. We may receive aggregated reports from Google, such as the number of adverts shown, which do not identify individual players.

5. APP STORES AND PURCHASES

Google Play handles downloads, updates and purchases under its own terms and privacy policy. In-app purchases are optional. Any purchase also removes adverts from the game; optional reward videos remain available.

When you make a purchase, Google Play processes the payment. The game receives confirmation of the purchase, such as the item bought and its order details, so that it can deliver your items and remove adverts. The game does not receive or ask for your payment card or banking details.

Google or your device manufacturer may separately process information about their services, including store use or diagnostics, according to their policies and your settings. This is separate from the game's local progress storage.

6. CONTACTING US

For privacy enquiries, contact ChilliCactus Games at: helpchillicactusgames@gmail.com

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message and any attachments. We use it to respond to your enquiry, investigate the issue or handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed.

For support correspondence, our lawful basis under UK data protection law is our legitimate interest in responding to players and resolving enquiries. We may also process information where necessary to meet a legal obligation. You do not have to contact us to play the game.

Correspondence may be processed by the email or support service you use to contact us and by the providers we use to receive and manage messages. We do not sell your personal information or use support messages for advertising. We may disclose information where required by law.

7. RETENTION, DELETION AND SECURITY

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you remove them or the app removes its stored data. To delete this information, use your Android device's Settings to clear Pocket Collider's app storage. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data as well.

Deleting local data removes saved progress, discovered particles and settings. Reset Progress in the game's settings also deletes your saved progress. We do not hold a server copy of your game save and cannot restore one for you. The Android app is configured to exclude its game data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Whether adverts have been removed is stored only on your device. Reset Progress keeps it, but clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it, and it cannot be restored, including on the same Google account or another device. Google Play keeps its own record of your payments.

Information collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's own policies. You can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

If you contact us, we keep correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and any related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal requirements. You can ask us to delete information you have sent us.

Local game data uses the storage protections provided by Android. The game does not add separate encryption to saved progress. The security of that storage also depends on the security of your device.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information we hold about you, and to receive a portable copy where that right applies.

You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Contact us using the details in section 6 to make a request. We cannot remotely access or delete a game save stored only on your device; use the deletion options in section 7 for that information. For information collected for advertising, you can also use the choices in section 4.

You may complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The game does not ask players to provide a name, email address, age or other personal details, and it does not transmit gameplay records to us. It contains no in-game chat. Pocket Collider is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so that we can address the matter and delete the information where appropriate.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy if the game's features or information-handling practices change. We will revise the date above and provide any additional notice required by applicable law. This policy describes the current game; changes involving accounts, online services, other advertising providers or new data collection would need to be reflected in an updated policy.
