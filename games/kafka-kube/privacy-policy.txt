  Kafka Kube - Privacy Policy
Developer: ChilliCactus Games
Applies to: Kafka Kube version 2.0 and later for Android
Last updated: 29 October 2026

Summary
Kafka Kube does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no
advertising, no analytics, no accounts and no internet access.

1. Information we collect
None. Kafka Kube does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device
identifiers or any other personal information, and it requests no Android permissions.

2. Information stored on your device
The app saves your game progress (levels unlocked, the kube skins you have chosen) and your
settings, such as sound on or off.
This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent
to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings,
deletes it.

3. Internet access, advertising and analytics
The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the
internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash-reporting
services and no third-party SDKs. Earlier versions of the game that showed ads or used other
online services have been replaced by this version.

Links
The settings screen has a link to ChilliCactus Games' Instagram page. Tapping it opens the page
in your browser or the Instagram app, outside Kafka Kube. Instagram's own privacy policy then
applies; the game itself sends nothing.

4. Google Play
The app is downloaded through Google Play. Google may collect information about downloads and
installs under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). ChilliCactus
Games does not receive personal information from Google about individual players.

5. Children
The game is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it
collects none from children.

6. Changes to this policy
If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and
the "Last updated" date above changed.

7. Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent to ChilliCactus Games at
helpchillicactusgames@gmail.com.
