BRANCH LINE PRIVACY POLICY
ChilliCactus Games
Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. ABOUT THIS POLICY

Branch Line is a railway puzzle game published by ChilliCactus Games
("we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled
in the Android version of the game.

Branch Line works offline. You do not need an account, and the game does
not send your gameplay records to us. The current game contains no
advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game saves information locally so that you can continue playing and
keep your preferences. This includes:

- Completed and skipped levels, stars, unlocked content and your current
  position in the game.
- Unfinished puzzle layouts and markings.
- Time spent on campaign levels, used to make the Skip button available.
- Daily puzzle dates, start and finish times, results and completion history.
- Endless best scores, earned achievement stamps and placement totals.
- Tutorial and unlock-notification records.
- Music, sound, display and motion preferences.

This information is stored locally within the Android app. It is used to
run the game, restore progress, time puzzles, award achievements and
remember settings. It is not sent to a ChilliCactus Games server.

Daily puzzles are generated on your device from the date. The game does
not contact a server to obtain a puzzle or submit your score. There are
no online accounts, public leaderboards or developer-operated cloud saves.

The game does not use advertising identifiers. Its local storage supports
game functions rather than advertising or tracking.

3. DEVICE ACCESS AND CONNECTIONS

The current Android app does not request access to your location,
contacts, camera, microphone, photos or messages. It has no Android
internet permission and loads its game files, artwork and music locally.

4. OPTIONAL SCORE SHARING

You can choose to share a Daily Line result. The prepared message includes
the puzzle date, board size, world, completion time and whether it is a
replay. Sharing is optional and is not required to play.

If you choose a sharing option, the message is passed to your selected
app or service, such as a messaging app, WhatsApp or an email app. Opening
a sharing service may disclose the prepared text to that service before
you send it to a recipient. That service's privacy policy and settings
apply. Branch Line does not read your contacts or choose recipients.

We do not receive a copy of your shared result through the game.

5. APP STORES AND PURCHASES

Google Play handles downloads, updates and any purchases under its own
terms and privacy policy. The game does not
ask for your payment card or banking details.

Google or your device manufacturer may
separately process information about their services, including store use
or diagnostics, according to their policies and your settings. This is
separate from the game's local progress storage.

6. CONTACTING US

For privacy enquiries, contact ChilliCactus Games at:
helpchillicactusgames@gmail.com

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide,
such as your email address, message and any attachments. We use it to
respond to your enquiry, investigate the issue or handle your request.
Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed.

For support correspondence, our lawful basis under UK data protection
law is our legitimate interest in responding to players and resolving
enquiries. We may also process information where necessary to meet a
legal obligation. You do not have to contact us to play the game.

Correspondence may be processed by the email or support service you use
to contact us and by the providers we use to receive and manage messages.
We do not sell your personal information or use support messages for
advertising. We may disclose information where required by law.

7. RETENTION, DELETION AND SECURITY

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you remove them
or the app removes its stored data. To delete this information, use your
Android device's Settings to clear Branch Line's app storage.
Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data as well.

Deleting local data removes saved progress, scores, stamps and settings.
Resetting an individual puzzle does not delete all game data. We do not
hold a server copy of your game save and cannot restore one for you.
The Android app is configured to exclude its game data from Android
cloud backup and device transfer.

If you contact us, we keep correspondence only for as long as needed to
resolve the enquiry and any related follow-up, or to meet applicable
legal requirements. You can ask us to delete information you have sent us.

Local game data uses the storage protections provided by Android.
The game does not add separate encryption to saved progress.
The security of that storage also depends on the security of your device.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct,
delete or restrict the use of personal information we hold about you,
and to receive a portable copy where that right applies.

You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
Contact us using the details in section 6 to make a request. We cannot
remotely access or delete a game save stored only on your device; use
the deletion options in section 7 for that information.

You may complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK,
this is the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The game does not ask players to provide a name, email address, age or
other personal details, and it does not transmit gameplay records to us.
It contains no advertising or in-game chat. If you believe a child has
provided personal information directly to us, contact us so that we can
address the matter and delete the information where appropriate.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy if the game's features or information-handling
practices change. We will revise the date above and provide any additional
notice required by applicable law. This policy describes the current
game; changes involving accounts, online services or new data collection
would need to be reflected in an updated policy.
