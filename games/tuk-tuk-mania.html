Tuk-Tuk Mania

Weave your tuk-tuk through India's busiest streets.












Hold your finger on the screen and drag left and right to weave a speeding tuk-tuk through traffic, cows and crossing carts. One bump and your run is over!

Collect the 5 stars in every city, placed on its trickiest stretches.

Reach 100% and keep going: the road changes every time and gets faster and faster.

Spend your coins at the gumball machine and collect 150 snazzy tuk-tuks!
